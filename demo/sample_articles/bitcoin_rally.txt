Bitcoin Surges Past $100,000 as Fed Announces Digital Dollar Pilot

Bitcoin reached a historic milestone, breaking $100,000 for the first time amid news that the Federal Reserve will launch a Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) pilot program.

The announcement sent shockwaves through traditional financial markets, with major banks scrambling to accelerate their blockchain initiatives.

Key points:
- BTC: $102,450 (+23% in 24 hours)
- ETH: $5,890 (+18%)
- Total crypto market cap: $4.2 trillion

JPMorgan analysts reversed their bearish stance, now projecting Bitcoin could reach $250,000 within 18 months as institutional adoption accelerates.
