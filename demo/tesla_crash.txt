Tesla Stock Plunges 15% After Surprise Recall Announcement

Tesla shares dropped dramatically in after-hours trading following an emergency recall affecting 2.3 million vehicles worldwide. The recall addresses concerns with the autonomous driving system after three fatal accidents were linked to software malfunctions.

CEO Elon Musk tweeted: "Safety is our top priority. We're issuing an immediate over-the-air update and offering full refunds to any concerned customers."

Analysts at Goldman Sachs downgraded the stock from "Buy" to "Hold," citing increasing regulatory scrutiny and competition from Chinese EV makers.

Market impact: $45 billion in market cap erased in minutes.
